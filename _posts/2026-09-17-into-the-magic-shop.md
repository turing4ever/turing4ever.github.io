[Into the Magic Shop](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/316170/into-the-magic-shop-by-james-doty-md/) is a memoir by neurosurgeon James R. Doty. At twelve, growing up in poverty with a troubled family, he wandered into a magic shop and met a woman named Ruth. She taught him exercises to calm his mind, open his heart, and imagine a different future.
